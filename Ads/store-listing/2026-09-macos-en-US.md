## Description

Reinstalling builds, resetting permissions and repeating simulator setup by hand gets old. CosmoKit puts those jobs in one Mac app, so you can prepare, exercise and verify iOS apps without leaving your desk.

**Free plan:** 1 simulator, 1 app profile, 5 saved items per tool, and watermarked captures. No account needed.

**CosmoKit Pro** adds unlimited simulators, profiles and saved items, unwatermarked captures, network conditions, and the full developer workflow. Monthly is $2.99 after a 14-day free trial, yearly is $19.99, or lifetime is $69.99.

**Reset & reinstall**
- Boot, shut down and switch between iOS simulators.
- Reinstall builds, reset app data and launch with a repeatable context.

**Capture**
- Capture screenshots and record video with frames, touch overlays and audio.

**Network**
- Proxy simulator traffic and shape latency, bandwidth and packet loss.
- Apply Edge, 3G or LTE presets, or create rules per host.

**Push & links**
- Send push notifications and open deep links or universal links.
- Simulate GPS locations for location-based app testing.

**Permissions & defaults**
- Inspect and change permissions and UserDefaults without terminal commands.

Requirements: macOS 14.7 or later and Xcode installed.
